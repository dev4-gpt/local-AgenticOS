# Brandeur Cloud OS Intake Workshop

**Goal:** Turn the Brandeur Cloud OS strategy into a signed v0 Client Edition
Manifest, a pilot workflow, and a safe cloud implementation brief.

**Attendees:** Brandeur owner, account lead, strategist, creative lead, web
lead, Veloce operator, and the pilot-project owner where appropriate.

## Pre-work: provide before the workshop

- Current B2B transformation proposal, onboarding form, scope template, and
  revision/approval policy.
- One selected pilot project with written permission to use its material inside
  the private Brandeur edition.
- Three examples each of approved strategy, identity, website, and content
  deliverables.
- Brandeur brand guideline, logo assets, font licences, colour references, and
  website/domain requirements.
- Current project-management, file-storage, and communication tools.
- Baseline delivery timings and recurring sources of delay or rework.

## 120-minute workshop agenda

### 1. Outcome and scope: 15 minutes

- What must be measurably better after the pilot?
- Which B2B programme stages are in scope?
- Which workflows are explicitly outside v0?

**Decision:** Approve the v0 capability allowlist and exclusions.

### 2. Current delivery map: 25 minutes

- Trace one recent project from lead to final handoff.
- Identify every source document, reviewer, handoff, revision, and external
  action.
- Mark where context is lost, decisions become unclear, or approval stalls.

**Decision:** Approve the target stages and named stage owners.

### 3. Context and knowledge: 20 minutes

- Identify the minimum intake material required for a grounded brand brief.
- Decide which work remains internal and which artefacts a client may see.
- Define evidence, citation, and versioning expectations.

**Decision:** Approve the pilot vault/data taxonomy and client-view boundary.

### 4. Creative, website, and approval flow: 25 minutes

- Define what strategic approval unlocks identity, website, communication, and
  delivery work.
- Define review criteria and maximum revision rounds for each stage.
- Decide whether the pilot needs direct client-approver accounts.

**Decision:** Approve the review gates and client approval experience.

### 5. Cloud, credentials, and cost: 20 minutes

- Confirm billing owner, GCP organisation/project, domain, and identity route.
- Select one initial text-model provider, model allowlist, cap, and overage
  approver.
- Confirm retention, backup, support access, and offboarding expectations.

**Decision:** Approve the cloud and credential prerequisites, without sharing
any actual secret during the workshop.

### 6. Pilot and success metrics: 15 minutes

- Select the first client engagement and required permissions.
- Capture baseline timing, revision, approval, reuse, and spend metrics.
- Set launch criteria, pilot review date, and named decision makers.

**Decision:** Approve the pilot charter and go/no-go criteria.

## Workshop outputs

- Updated BRANDEUR-CLIENT-EDITION-MANIFEST.yaml
- Confirmed v0 capability matrix and exclusions
- Pilot workflow with owners and approval gates
- Cloud/identity/provider prerequisite list
- Evidence-backed acceptance plan and baseline scorecard

## Do not collect in this document

- API keys, OAuth tokens, passwords, session cookies, or personal credentials
- Client confidential material without a signed permission basis
- Unbounded permissions to publish, spend, send messages, or execute code
