# Focused AgentOS buyer-scenario seed for MiroFish

This scenario is for **hypothesis generation, not market validation**. AgentOS is a proposed governed AI workspace; its complete current-release customer journey has not been certified. Do not imply that the customer workflow, hosted isolation, citations, approvals, or costs have passed a live end-to-end acceptance test.

## Customer hypothesis

The initial segment to investigate is a small AI automation or software-development consultancy serving multiple clients. A candidate repeated workflow is turning approved client documents and a scoped request into a client-ready proposal, audit, or weekly report. The proposed product outcome is a source-linked first draft, kept within the selected client's context and reviewed by a human before use.

This is a hypothesis, not established customer language or evidence. The most frequent deliverable, time spent, error cost, current alternatives, buyer, and willingness to pay are unknown. The simulation should preserve those unknowns and allow every persona to say that the problem does not occur or is not important.

## Scenario boundaries

- Restrict the fictional cohort to solo operators and teams of 2-20 in agency delivery.
- Include five AI-literacy levels, with five workflow owners and five security reviewers. These dimensions are scenario coverage, not a representative sample.
- Keep client-context separation, source traceability, and human review as claims to investigate, not capabilities to presume.
- Use the current published offer only as a stated scenario input: $2,500 one-time setup plus $1,250/month for the platform, with provider usage billed separately. Do not invent a discount, free tier, included provider spend, or customer commitment.
- A persona whose stated budget does not clearly cover the offer should reject, defer, or request evidence; a synthetic response is not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Ask what the buyer does today, which deliverable recurs, how often, how long it takes, what failure costs, and what proof is needed before a pilot. Do not create legal, medical, financial, or compliance advice as product output.
- Do not send real customer documents, credentials, or personal data into the simulation. Keep Zep graph-memory writes disabled for this pilot.

## Interpretation

Use generated posts only to inspect whether the prompt surfaces relevant workflow questions and objections. They are not interviews, demand, conversion, pricing, or market-size evidence. Human review is required before any generated content is used externally, and real buyer interviews remain a separate acceptance gate.
