# Deferred Next Goal: Multiplayer AI and Compliance Infrastructure

## Status

Deferred. Do not begin implementation or transition goals until the current AgentOS production-readiness goal is complete, its evidence is reviewed, and the user explicitly authorizes this next goal.

## Direction

Explore combining two adjacent product directions:

- **Multiplayer AI:** let authorized teammates join the same live agent session to observe progress, redirect work, approve actions, and hand work off, with clear ownership and a durable activity history.
- **AI-native compliance infrastructure:** make policies and controls operational through scoped evidence, provenance, audit trails, budgets, approvals, and eventually control-to-requirement mapping and renewal workflows.

The intended combination is collaborative AI work with governance built into the session, not a claim that AgentOS already provides compliance coverage.

## Validation and boundaries

- Treat this as a product hypothesis, not a committed roadmap or shipped capability.
- Before implementation, select and validate a specific customer workflow and buyer; the current small AI/development consultancy hypothesis is a candidate, not a settled choice.
- Define the initial regulatory and jurisdictional scope with qualified review before making compliance claims.
- Do not start a new goal, product pivot, implementation, or deployment from this note alone.
