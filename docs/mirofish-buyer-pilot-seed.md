# AgentOS buyer-scenario seed for MiroFish

AgentOS is a proposed governed workspace for AI-assisted, context-heavy work. It has a local operator edition and a hosted tenant edition. The intended public journey is request with consent, email verification, reviewed payment, human manifest review, isolated tenant provisioning, activation, provider setup, a first useful action, usage controls, support, export, and offboarding. The complete current-release journey has **not** yet been certified. Do not treat any step as proven merely because it is described here.

The product aims to keep tenant data separate, put execution behind provider/model allowlists and budget controls, and preserve artifacts and decisions. Customers may bring their own provider credentials; managed-provider access requires team approval and a budget decision. External publishing and outreach require human approval. The current public base offer is $2,500 one-time setup plus $1,250/month for the platform, with provider usage billed separately. Scope additions and final terms require an approved quote and signed agreement; do not infer a discount, free tier, or included provider credits. This is the published offer, not evidence that checkout or activation works end to end.

Potential buyer motivations include reducing repeated client handoffs, retaining project context, governing AI use, and making outputs auditable. Potential objections include setup time, unclear first value, data privacy, compliance, provider costs, trust in agent outputs, and uncertainty about who owns support and failures. A buyer may reject the product or choose a simpler existing tool.

Simulate a prospective buyer evaluating the product, trying to understand a concrete first workflow, and deciding whether to request a scoped pilot. Include hands-on users, workflow owners, technical evaluators, security reviewers, and budget holders across agency delivery, software startups, ecommerce, local services, healthcare administration, education, nonprofit operations, finance operations, legal services, and creative media. Vary organization size, AI literacy from novice to expert, budget, privacy sensitivity, and region. Include skeptical and nonbuyer personas. Compare each persona's budget with both the setup fee and monthly base fee. A persona whose budget cannot cover the published offer should normally reject or defer, not imagine a cheaper plan; an "over $1000" monthly band alone does not prove it can cover $1,250/month or setup. Do not create medical, legal, or financial advice as a product outcome.

The questions to investigate are: Which first-use workflows are legible and valuable? Where does the customer journey lose trust or momentum? What evidence would each buyer need before paying? Which labels or screens mislead low-AI-literacy users? Which privacy, governance, and cost controls are insufficient? What alternative product or manual process would they choose instead?

Current product evidence for the scenario, observed on October 3, 2026: the public front door and the authenticated tenant workspace are separate sites. The public app domain loads, but its shared login route returns 404. The configured local client preview has a signed-in session and loads 24 Memory records, 29 graph nodes, 57 edges, 9 notebook records, workflow history, and 2 historical delivery packages. These observations demonstrate reading existing records, not completing a new customer workflow. Department workflow creation is disabled in that local runtime's configuration. The local candidate displays a reviewed source-pack import control that was absent from the older signed-in cloud test workspace. Importing the full course and verifying a current cloud customer journey remain incomplete. Do not assume these experiences are unified, that historical successes certify this release, or that the new import is already deployed.

This document contains no customer data, secrets, or claim of market validation. Synthetic behavior must be reported as hypotheses and compared with real interviews and measured usage.
