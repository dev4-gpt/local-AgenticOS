# Personal video Content OS — Phase 1 manual pilot

**Status:** staged in Veloce AgenticOS; manual assembly only.

This implements the structure of the user-provided [AI Video Generation
Strategy Blueprint](https://share.gemini.google/VGI8JH19QeFI) without connecting
Gemini Flow/Veo, HeyGen, ElevenLabs, social networks, a scheduler, or analytics.
It uses the existing Hermes video content factory so each deliverable is
tenant-scoped, researched, quality-checked, and held for a human approval.

## The first loop

1. Capture a portfolio-video brief and the owner-approved public source links.
2. Research and verify factual claims against those sources.
3. Produce hook alternatives, a 30–60 second script, a visual/cut plan, caption
   copy, and a platform-ready description/CTA as reviewable work.
4. Assemble the first three to five videos manually with the chosen tools.
5. Review technical accuracy, pacing, captions, portfolio links, owner
   likeness/voice consent, and synthetic-media disclosure.
6. Record an explicit approval. The owner then posts natively; Veloce may only
   record that delivery after approval.

The preferred micro-story is hook (0–3 seconds), architecture (3–15 seconds),
real demo (15–30 seconds), and CTA (30–45 seconds). Every script must include
the exact portfolio link destination and a verification source for claims.

## What stays disabled

- Gemini Flow/Veo image or video generation
- HeyGen avatar creation or rendering
- ElevenLabs voice cloning or narration
- Social account authorization, scheduling, direct posting, or messaging
- Automatic platform-analytics imports

Those are separate activation decisions. Do not add a credential to a Veloce
brief or repository. Every later provider connector needs its own terms review,
owner consent, spend cap, data-retention/deletion policy, disclosure review,
and a rollback drill.

## What I need from the owner to begin Phase 1

1. Choose one portfolio project and provide its public links, live demo, and
   factual claims that may be made.
2. State whether the pilot may use your likeness, voice, both, or neither—and
   confirm that the materials are yours to use.
3. Give the preferred destination(s), audience, tone, and portfolio CTA URL.
4. Approve a first script and visual plan before any outside creative tool is
   used.

Until those inputs are supplied, this is planning infrastructure—not an active
media-generation or publishing system.
