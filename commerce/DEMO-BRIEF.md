# Fictional studio website brief

This is synthetic demonstration material written for Veloce Commerce. There is no real client, service agreement or customer validation.

A fictional independent design studio wants one responsive page introducing its services. The page must contain a headline, a services section and an inquiry link. The inquiry link opens the visitor's email application; a contact form and backend are outside this scope.

The deliverable is an HTML and CSS source bundle with local preview instructions. Public hosting is optional and is not included in the requested deliverable. Include one round of content revisions. Do not promise a launch date: the studio has not chosen its headline, service descriptions, inquiry email address or delivery schedule. Ask for those decisions before treating them as agreed.
